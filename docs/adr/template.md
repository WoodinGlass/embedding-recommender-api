## Context

What is the situation, and what forces are at play? Keep it short. State the
problem in terms a reader three months from now can understand without
reading the whole codebase.

Constraints worth naming explicitly:

- Scale (catalog size, RPS, latency budget)
- Team and operational reality (single-node deploy, on-call capacity)
- Existing decisions this builds on (`docs/decisions.md` section numbers)

## Decision

What we decided, in one or two sentences. Present tense. No hedging: an ADR
that says "we might consider..." is a note, not a decision.

## Alternatives considered

The "why not" column is what makes an ADR useful in a review. A record that
says "we chose the best option" without naming the others is not evidence of
thinking; it is a claim.
